Optional --no-update-format flag to prevent "conflict_with" lines from being removed from the .rb.

Possibility to specify a semver instead of a commit SHA, by searching in commit messages, or optionally in the .rb file to be sure to get the latest commit for that version...

Possibility to pass other options to the final brew install or brew reinstall command, such as --force...
